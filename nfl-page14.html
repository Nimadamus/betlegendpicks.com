Chiefs vs Ravens

Thursday, Sep 4 | 8:20 PM ET | NBC | Arrowhead Stadium
KC: Super Bowl Champs | BAL: AFC #1 Seed | Spread: KC -3 | O/U: 47.5

The 2025 NFL season kicks off with a rematch of last year's AFC Championship Game. The defending Super Bowl champion Chiefs host the Ravens at Arrowhead Stadium for what promises to be an electric Thursday night opener.

Chiefs Dynasty Continues

Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs are going for their third consecutive Super Bowl championship - something no team has accomplished in NFL history. The offense returns all its key pieces, and the defense has only gotten stronger. Kansas City is the overwhelming favorite to repeat as champions.

Ravens Revenge Tour

Baltimore enters with a chip on their shoulder after falling short in the AFC title game. Lamar Jackson is coming off another MVP-caliber season, and the Ravens added weapons to help him in the playoffs. This is their chance to make a statement on the biggest stage.

Opening Night Pick

The Chiefs have won 13 straight home openers. While the Ravens have the firepower to keep this close, Arrowhead's energy on banner night typically gives Kansas City an edge. Look for a tight, entertaining game decided in the fourth quarter.

Week 1 Full Slate Preview

Following Thursday's opener, Week 1 features a loaded slate of games:

  • Sunday 1:00 PM: Multiple divisional matchups
  • Sunday 4:25 PM: Marquee NFC showdowns
  • Sunday Night: Eagles vs Packers in Brazil
  • Monday Night: 49ers vs Jets doubleheader
