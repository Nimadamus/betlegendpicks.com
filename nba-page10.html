Young Stars Collide

Two of the NBA's most exciting young rosters meet in Cleveland. The Thunder have emerged as a legitimate contender in the Western Conference, while the Cavaliers have been one of the league's pleasant surprises.

Thunder Analysis

Oklahoma City's young core has taken a significant leap. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander leads an offense that can score in bunches, while the defense has improved dramatically. The Thunder are no longer a team of the future - they're competing now.

Cavaliers Analysis

Cleveland has built a formidable team around their core players. The Cavaliers' defense has been stifling, and their offense, while not elite, is efficient. At home, Cleveland is a tough out for any opponent.
