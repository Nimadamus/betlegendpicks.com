Yankees-Red Sox Rivalry

The greatest rivalry in baseball continued throughout the 2025 season. These two AL East powerhouses met multiple times, with each series carrying significant divisional implications.

Season Context

Both teams entered the 2025 season with championship aspirations. The Yankees, as always, were loaded with power hitters and had made pitching additions in the offseason. The Red Sox looked to their young core to take the next step in their development.

Historical Note: Yankees-Red Sox games at Fenway Park have historically featured higher scoring totals, with the Green Monster playing a significant factor in game outcomes.
