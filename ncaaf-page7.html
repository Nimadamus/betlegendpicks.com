Red River Rivalry

The Texas Longhorns and Oklahoma Sooners meet at the Cotton Bowl in Dallas for one of college football's greatest rivalries. The neutral-site showdown at the State Fair of Texas is always one of the most anticipated games of the season.

Oklahoma Outlook

Oklahoma is looking to prove themselves against their historic rival. The Sooners have talent and are eager to pull off an upset on the big stage. The split crowd at the Cotton Bowl ensures both teams will have passionate support.
