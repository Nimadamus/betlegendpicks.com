Season Preview

The 2025-26 college basketball season begins with intrigue across the landscape. The transfer portal has reshaped rosters, elite freshmen are ready to make immediate impacts, and returning players look to lead their programs to championship glory.

Preseason Rankings

The preseason polls establish early narratives, but the beauty of college basketball is that rankings mean nothing until games are played. November serves as the first test of how teams will perform.

Early Season Betting Approach

Opening week games present unique challenges for bettors. New roster constructions, untested lineups, and unknown chemistry make handicapping difficult. Sharp bettors often use early games to gather information rather than risk significant units.

Opening Week Note: Teams with significant roster turnover typically need 10-15 games to establish their true identity.

November Tournament Season

Early-season tournaments like the Maui Invitational, Battle 4 Atlantis, and Champions Classic provide marquee matchups and resume-building opportunities.
