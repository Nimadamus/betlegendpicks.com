SEC West Showdown

One of college football's greatest rivalries renews as Alabama travels to Death Valley to face LSU. This November night game in Baton Rouge is one of the most anticipated matchups of the season, with major CFP implications on the line.

Alabama Outlook

The Crimson Tide enter this matchup looking to continue their dominance in the SEC. Alabama's offense has been explosive, and their defense, while young, has shown flashes of the typical Alabama standard. Road games in Death Valley are always a challenge, and the team will need to handle the hostile environment.

LSU Outlook

Playing at home under the lights, LSU has a significant environment advantage. The Tigers have built momentum through their season and will feed off the energy of one of the most intimidating crowds in college football. Their offensive firepower makes them dangerous against any opponent.

Night Game Factor: LSU is historically dominant in night games at Tiger Stadium, with the crowd creating one of college football's most challenging atmospheres.

New Big Ten Rivalry

USC makes the trip to The Big House in a matchup that showcases the new look of the Big Ten Conference. This cross-country showdown features two blue-blood programs meeting in a conference game for the first time in the modern era.

Game Analysis

Michigan's physical style of play at home presents a challenge for USC's offense. The Wolverines' ability to control the line of scrimmage and run the football has been their identity. USC will need to match that physicality while also exploiting any openings in the passing game.

Weather Watch: November games in Ann Arbor can feature cold temperatures that may affect passing offenses not accustomed to playing in those conditions.

Week 10 Slate Overview

Beyond the marquee matchups, Week 10 features crucial games across all Power 4 conferences:

SEC Action

Multiple conference games with division title implications. The SEC race continues to be competitive, with several teams still in the hunt for the conference championship game.

Big 12 Battles

The expanded Big 12 features competitive matchups throughout its schedule, with the conference race still wide open entering November.

ACC Showdowns

Conference title implications are on the line as teams jockey for position in the ACC standings.
